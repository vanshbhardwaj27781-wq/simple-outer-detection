SIMPLE OUTLIER DETECTION - SHORT INTERPRETATION

Dataset: Retail Sales
Column checked: Sales

Method used:
I used the IQR (Interquartile Range) method.

Q1 = 2425.00
Q3 = 7375.00
IQR = 4950.00
Lower Bound = -5000.00

Number of outliers found: 7

Interpretation:
The IQR method identified unusually high Sales values outside the calculated limits.
The records are marked as High Outlier in the outlier table. These values should not
be removed automatically. They should first be checked because a high value can be
a genuine large sale rather than an error.

For this sample dataset, the very high sales values are possible large retail orders.
Therefore, I would investigate the original transaction details before deciding whether
to remove, correct, or keep them.

Interview questions:
1. What is an outlier?
An outlier is a value that is unusually high or low compared with most other values.

2. Should every outlier be removed?
No. An outlier should be investigated first. It may be a valid observation or a data error.
